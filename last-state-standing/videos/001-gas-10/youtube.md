## Descrição
What happens if gas hits $10 a gallon and stays there? We took two official numbers for every state, gallons of gasoline sold per person (U.S. Energy Information Administration, 2024) and income per person (Bureau of Economic Analysis, 2025), and ranked all 50 states by how much of their income $10 gas would eat.

#1 Alabama: 10.6% of income. #2 Mississippi: 10.4%. #50 New York: 2.9%.

Method: gallons sold per person × $10 ÷ per capita personal income. Gasoline is counted where it is sold, so states with heavy through-traffic look slightly thirstier than their residents are. Historical prices: EIA weekly U.S. regular retail price (via FRED). Inflation adjustment: CPI-U.

Sources:
EIA State Energy Data System (SEDS), motor gasoline consumption and population, 2024
BEA per capita personal income by state, 2025 (via FRED, series [ST]PCPI)
EIA U.S. Regular All Formulations Retail Gasoline Prices (FRED: GASREGW)
