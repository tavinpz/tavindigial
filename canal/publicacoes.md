## 001 · Short · Why Isn't the Netherlands Underwater?
- Arquivo: concrete_atlas_short_01_netherlands.mp4 (45 s)
- Título: Why Isn't the Netherlands Underwater?
- Descrição:

A quarter of the Netherlands sits below sea level. After the 1953 North Sea flood killed 1,836 people, the Dutch built the Delta Works: 13 dams and storm barriers that now protect the country from a once-in-10,000-year storm.

Sources: Wikipedia (North Sea flood of 1953), Rijkswaterstaat.
