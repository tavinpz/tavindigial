## Descrição do canal
How the world builds against nature.

Concrete Atlas explains megaprojects, cities and the geography that shapes them, with maps, data and engineering, told in minutes. Why a country lives below the sea. Why a city is sinking. How a canal moved the world's trade.

New Shorts every day and a deep dive every week. Every fact comes with its source.
