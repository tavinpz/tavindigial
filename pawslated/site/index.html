3 of the 30 signs inside

The yawn

A big yawn when he isn't tired, often during a hug.

"This is too much for me."

The head tilt

Head cocked to one side when you talk to him.

"Wait, did you say a word I know?"

The lean

He presses his whole body into your legs and stays.

"You're my person. This is a hug."
