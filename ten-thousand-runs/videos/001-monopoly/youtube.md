## Descrição
We built Monopoly in code, with the official rules, every Chance and Community Chest card, doubles and jail, and played it 10,000 times to find out which properties actually win.

What we found:
• Jail is the busiest square on the board (10.5% of all moves end there), and the orange set sits right where people walk out of it.
• The orange set won 50% of its games, twice its fair share. Park Place and Boardwalk won only 17%.
• The third house is where the money doubles.
• Paying $50 to leave jail early made players lose more often.

How we ran it: 4-player games, standard US board and rents, 32 houses and 12 hotels, all 32 cards, three-doubles-to-jail, $200 for passing GO. Each player started with one full color set; everything else was bought on landing. No trades and no auctions. 10,000 games for the main test, plus 1,000 head-to-head games for every pair of sets and 10,000 games for each strategy test.
